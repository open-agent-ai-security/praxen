=== LANE FIT NOTES ===
  r1: Strained in three places. (1) uagents is a framework, not a deployed agent: user_inputs holds peer agents, a self-declared user-address class and the operator rather than human end users, and the agent's real application handlers are operator code that does not exist in this tree. (2) The outbound dispenser/resolver, the mailbox client and the registration policies are in-process library modules, but they are the channel connectors to Almanac, Agentverse and peers, so they sit in client_adapters rather than agent_core — the split between 'adapter' and 'core' is a judgement call here, not a structural fact. (3) The wallet is placed in tools_mcp as the capability handlers invoke, though it is an in-process property of the Agent object; and the inspector endpoints are carved out of agent.py into client_adapters because their trust consequence is a control plane, not orchestration.
  r2: Two strains. First, this target has no LLM on its dominant path — the agent_core lane is filled by protocol plumbing, a signature control and two state stores rather than a prompt-and-model loop, and the only model node lives in an opt-in experimental subpackage (uagents/experimental/chat_agent) that the frozen findings never examined; its three nodes and the model-egress / tool-invocation boundaries are code-cited but describe a surface most deployments never turn on. Second, client_adapters had to absorb both directions of the network edge: asgi.py and mailbox.py are inbound ingress surfaces and communication.py is the outbound sender, none of which is a client the user talks to, but they are adapters rather than core loop code so the lane is the least-bad fit. registration.py and resolver.py went to tools_mcp because the remit's Allowed Tools list names registration and address resolution as capabilities, though neither is a tool in the model-invoked sense.
