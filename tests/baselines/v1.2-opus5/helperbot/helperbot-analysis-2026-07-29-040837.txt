BEHAVIOR SUMMARY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The dominant pattern is a codebase that ships working safety primitives and
wires none of them to this agent: detectAttacks() classifies every incoming
message, maybeEnforce() in aim-enforcer.js denies out-of-grant tool actions
for sibling agents, and prompts.js contains a hardened prompt for SecureBot —
while HelperBot's own branch of generateResponseImpl() answers a detected
injection with "New instructions accepted" and answers a system-prompt probe
by echoing its persona. Because createAgentServer() applies no authentication,
sets wildcard CORS and binds all interfaces, those behaviors are reachable in
one unauthenticated hop from anywhere the port is published, and the only
record of the attempt is a 500-entry in-memory ring buffer that dies with the
process. The result is a remotely triggerable goal hijack and
system-prompt-plus-credential disclosure with no forensic trail — the three
findings that compose that chain are cross-linked in the register.
